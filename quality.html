Quality and Inspection Capabilities

Equipped with state-of-the-art metrology instruments, certified personnel, and rigorous aerospace compliance standards.

Precision Metrology

CMM — Carl Zeiss

Our Carl Zeiss CONTURA G2 CMM supports precision verification of complex geometries, critical dimensions and geometric tolerances. With measurement accuracy ranging from ±3 µm to ±10 µm, depending on measurement conditions and application, we support first-piece validation, production inspection and final acceptance. Controlled inspection routines, suitable fixturing and documented results provide reliable evidence of conformity to customer drawings.

Optical Inspection

VMM — Omega

Our Omega Vision Measurement Machine, with a measuring range of 250 × 150 × 150 mm, provides non-contact dimensional inspection of fine profiles, small features, hole patterns and delicate edges. Optical measurement enables verification without probe contact, supporting inspection of features that are difficult to access using conventional instruments. Controlled lighting, suitable magnification and calibrated measurement settings support repeatable, documented results.

Surface Finish

Surface Roughness Testing — Mitutoyo

Our Mitutoyo surface roughness tester measures Ra and Rz parameters to verify machined surface finish against drawing and customer requirements. From functional faces to critical mating surfaces, it supports machining-process control and final inspection. Documented measurements help confirm finish conformity for components where surface texture influences fit, friction, sealing and performance.

Linear Measurement

Digital Height Gauges

Our digital height gauges support accurate measurement of heights, steps and dimensional relationships from established reference surfaces. Used with suitable surface plates, probes and fixtures, they provide efficient verification during setup, in-process inspection and final checks. Consistent measurement methods help identify dimensional variation early and maintain production conformity.

Calibrated Tooling

Standard Instruments

Our dimensional inspection capability is supported by precision measuring instruments selected to suit each feature and tolerance, including micrometers, vernier calipers, bore gauges, dial indicators and limit gauges, as applicable. Controlled calibration, instrument identification and appropriate handling support dependable measurements. These instruments enable practical verification throughout production, from incoming material checks to final component acceptance.

Expert Staff

Trained Inspectors

Our trained inspectors combine drawing interpretation, GD&T knowledge and practical measurement skills to verify precision-machined components. Inspection activities follow defined methods, acceptance criteria and documentation requirements, with attention to critical characteristics, traceability and surface condition. Coordination between quality and production teams helps identify deviations early, support corrective action and maintain consistent product quality.

Aerospace Planning

AS9145 APQP & PPAP

We support Advanced Product Quality Planning and Production Part Approval Process requirements under AS9145, helping customers move from product introduction to controlled series production. Our approach connects manufacturing feasibility, process flow, PFMEA, control plans, measurement-system evaluation and process-capability evidence, as applicable. Structured reviews and customer-specific approval documentation help establish production readiness and manage risks before full-scale manufacture.

First Article Inspection

AS9102C FAI

We support First Article Inspection to AS9102C, providing documented verification that the production process can produce components meeting specified requirements. FAI packages bring together part accountability, material and special-process evidence, ballooned drawings and characteristic-level inspection results. Full or partial FAI is prepared as applicable to new parts and relevant changes, providing traceable evidence for customer review and acceptance.
